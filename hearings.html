Court Hearings

The Driz matter is not one case. It is a tangle of them... a criminal prosecution, a $72 million civil judgment, a TMZ photographer’s own lawsuit, an eve-of-trial bankruptcy, and a fraudulent-transfer suit chasing the money. Across nearly a decade there have been venue changes, judge reassignments, and prosecutor swaps. This page tracks the moving pieces, color-coded by case, every entry drawn from the court record.

Alberto’s and Shawn’s assault charges end in post-disposition dismissal. Co-defendant Aaron Torbati convicted. Exact plea and sentencing dates pending minute orders.
2019-05-16 Suraci Suraci case settles Stanley Mosk Courthouse · Dept. 71 The court records an Order to Show Cause "after settlement as to Defendants Alberto Driz and Maurice Driz." The photographer has reached a deal.
2019-07-15 Suraci Suraci jury trial vacated Stanley Mosk Courthouse · Dept. 71 The trial, estimated at 7 to 10 days and coordinated with the Greene and Findel cases, is taken off calendar as the settlement is finalized.
2019-09-27 Suraci Stipulated judgment; Blaze Pizza dismissed Stanley Mosk Courthouse · Dept. 71 A hearing on the Stipulation for Judgment and Entry of Judgment is held; Blaze Pizza, LLC and the Doe defendants are dismissed. The case is disposed. The amount is not public.
Dec 2019 Civil Defendants’ attorney relieved Stanley Mosk · counsel Maria Hall withdrawn All four defendants proceed toward the civil trial representing themselves.
Jul 2021 (eve of trial) Bankruptcy Shawn Driz files Chapter 7 at 4:45 p.m. U.S. Bankruptcy Court, C.D. Cal. · No. 2:21-bk-15494-VZ An automatic stay is triggered in a bid to blow up the civil trial before it starts.
Jul 2021 (+2 days) Bankruptcy Bankruptcy stay lifted C.D. Cal. The civil trial goes ahead anyway.
Jul 2021 Civil Three-week jury trial and verdict Stanley Mosk The jury rejects every self-defense story and returns a verdict of $72,645,492 against all four defendants.
2021 Bankruptcy Nondischargeability adversary filed C.D. Cal. · No. 2:21-ap-01199 Plaintiffs move to keep the judgment alive as a debt for "willful and malicious injury," which bankruptcy cannot erase.
2021 Bankruptcy Trustee files Report of No Distribution C.D. Cal. Shawn Driz is recorded on the federal books as a "no asset" case.
2023-10-12 Koral Greene v. Koral filed LA Superior Court · No. 23STCV24928 · Dept. O · Judge H. Jay Ford III Alleges Alberto Driz fraudulently transferred his 49% Blaze Pizza stake to Peter Koral to look judgment-proof.
2024 Civil Judgment-debtor examinations Stanley Mosk · enforcement Plaintiffs compel the defendants to answer under oath about where their money is.
2024-04-25 Koral Demurrers overruled Dept. O · Judge H. Jay Ford III The fraudulent-transfer, civil-conspiracy, and unfair-competition claims survive. The case advances past pleadings.
2025-02-10 Koral Trial date set (per the 2024 ruling) Dept. O Current posture being confirmed.
2026-07-15 Civil Enforcement hearing held Dept. 729 (judgment enforcement) Ex parte on a claim-of-exemption timeliness issue; the motion is granted.
2026-11-16 Civil Claim of Exemption / Third-Party Claim hearing set Dept. 729 Pending. Plaintiffs are moving on assets whose ownership a third party disputes.
